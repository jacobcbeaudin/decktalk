DeckTalk turns a markdown script, HTML slides and your voice into one narrated mp4. You write the
words in `script.md` and the pictures in an HTML page. `cues.json` names the spoken phrase each
picture lands on, and the finished film is measured against that promise before you are told it is
built.

## Start here

Three pages take you from an empty machine to a section of your own.

- [Quickstart](/quickstart) installs DeckTalk and builds the starter film with no account and no spend.
- [Requirements and costs](/requirements) covers what a machine needs, what it downloads, and which commands spend money.
- [Your first deck](/guides/first-deck) writes one section across the four files, then checks, builds and verifies it.

## Understand the model

- [How it works](/concepts/how-it-works) walks the six stages, from the spoken takes to the measured film.
- [Cues](/concepts/cues) explains how a phrase from the script becomes a second on a section clock.
- [The page contract](/concepts/page-contract) is how a slide tells the recorder what it is doing, and [Design a slide](/guides/design-a-slide) turns that into a slide worth watching.
- [Writing for the ear](/guides/writing-for-the-ear) gives every picture a word worth landing on.
- [Sound](/concepts/sound) covers the music, the ambience bed and the effects.
