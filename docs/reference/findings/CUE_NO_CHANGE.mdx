Nothing in the picture changed at the cue's second, so the reveal never happened.

This finding is an error, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk check` reports it when it judges the project's files before a build, and `decktalk verify` when it measures the finished film and reads back each recording log.
Pass `--allow CUE_NO_CHANGE` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

The settings that move this verdict are `video.crf`, `verify.probe_delays_seconds`, `verify.probe_diff_luma`, `verify.changed_share_min_percent`, `verify.margin_min_points`.

## What to do

Look at the two frames the message names, then make the reveal bigger, or give the element a moment attribute for the cue that fired. `decktalk storyboard --before CUE --after CUE` freezes that pair.

Troubleshooting says more under [A picture that does not change](/help/troubleshooting#a-picture-that-does-not-change).
