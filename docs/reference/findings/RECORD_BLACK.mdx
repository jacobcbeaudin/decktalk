A recorded frame is black, so the film shows nothing at that moment.

This finding is an error, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk record` reports it when it records the pages, and `decktalk verify` when it measures the finished film and reads back each recording log.
Pass `--allow RECORD_BLACK` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

The settings that move this verdict are `verify.after_dip_seconds`, `verify.black_max_luma`.

## What to do

Open the page with `decktalk serve` and make it draw something visible at that second. A slide whose every element waits for a later cue shows nothing until the first one.

Troubleshooting says more under [A recording that opens black or runs short](/help/troubleshooting#a-recording-that-opens-black-or-runs-short).
