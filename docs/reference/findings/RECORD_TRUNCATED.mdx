A recording stopped before its section's clock ran out, so the film is short of picture.

This finding is an error, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk record` reports it when it records the pages, and `decktalk verify` when it measures the finished film and reads back each recording log.
Pass `--allow RECORD_TRUNCATED` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

## What to do

Record the section again with `decktalk record --section N`, because a short stepped recording is an encode that ended early.

Troubleshooting says more under [A recording that opens black or runs short](/help/troubleshooting#a-recording-that-opens-black-or-runs-short).
