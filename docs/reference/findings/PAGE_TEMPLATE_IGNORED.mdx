A template nested inside a slide declares no slide of its own, so nothing ever mounts it.

This finding is an error, and the page reports it from the browser. `decktalk check` reports it when it judges the project's files before a build, `decktalk record` when it records the pages, and `decktalk verify` when it measures the finished film and reads back each recording log.
Pass `--allow PAGE_TEMPLATE_IGNORED` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

## What to do

Give the nested template its own `data-slide`, or take it out of the slide.

Troubleshooting says more under [A page the contract refuses](/help/troubleshooting#a-page-the-contract-refuses).
