The mixed film misses the loudness it was mastered to.

This finding is a warning, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk assemble` reports it when it cuts and mixes the film.
Pass `--allow MIX_LOUDNESS` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

The settings that move this verdict are `audio.target_lufs`, `audio.true_peak_max_dbtp`.

## What to do

Judge loudness on a voiced build, then settle a bed that fights the voice with `[mix] music_db`, `music_duck_db` and `ambience_db` in decktalk.toml. Change `audio.target_lufs` only for a platform that asks for another.

Troubleshooting says more under [A loudness miss](/help/troubleshooting#a-loudness-miss).
