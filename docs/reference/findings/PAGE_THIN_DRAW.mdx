A frozen slide draws less of the picture than a change must cross to be seen.

This finding is a warning, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk check` reports it when it judges the project's files before a build.
Pass `--allow PAGE_THIN_DRAW` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

The settings that move this verdict are `video.crf`, `verify.changed_share_min_percent`.

## What to do

Make what the slide draws on its cue larger, such as a longer or thicker stroke, so the change crosses the floor `verify.changed_share_min_percent` sets.

Troubleshooting says more under [A picture that does not change](/help/troubleshooting#a-picture-that-does-not-change).
