A cue has no onset DeckTalk can measure: its phrase resolved to its section's start rather than to a word, or the picture changed at it and no frame rose as far as an onset must.

This finding is a warning, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk check` reports it when it judges the project's files before a build, `decktalk cue` when it resolves each cue phrase against the takes, and `decktalk verify` when it measures the finished film and reads back each recording log.
Pass `--allow CUE_NO_ONSET` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

The settings that move this verdict are `verify.onset_rise_points`, `verify.onset_diff_luma`.

## What to do

Anchor the cue on a word the section speaks, rather than on its start or an `offset_seconds` that pulls it there, or mark the row `"verify": false`. When the picture changed and no frame rose, make the change arrive faster, so one frame carries most of it.

Troubleshooting says more under [A late landing](/help/troubleshooting#a-late-landing).
