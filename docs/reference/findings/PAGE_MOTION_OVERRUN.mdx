A motion span runs past the measurable ceiling, so the cue it carries cannot be verified.

This finding is an error, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk check` reports it when it judges the project's files before a build.
Pass `--allow PAGE_MOTION_OVERRUN` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

The settings that move this verdict are `motion.scale`.

## What to do

Shorten `data-in-seconds` on the element, or lower `motion.scale`. Something meant to move for longer than half a second is declared as a motion or a loop.

Troubleshooting says more under [A late landing](/help/troubleshooting#a-late-landing).
