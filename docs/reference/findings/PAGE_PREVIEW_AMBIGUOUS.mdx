Two sections name one scene, so a preview cannot choose whose cue times to play.

This finding is an error, and the page reports it from the browser. `decktalk serve` reports it when a scene's preview plays in your browser, which says it in the page's console.
Pass `--allow PAGE_PREVIEW_AMBIGUOUS` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

## What to do

Give the two sections different scenes.
