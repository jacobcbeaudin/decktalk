The picture steps at a section cut, so the film pops on the seam.

This finding is an error, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk check` reports it when it judges the project's files before a build, and `decktalk verify` when it measures the finished film and reads back each recording log.
Pass `--allow CUT_POP` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

The settings that move this verdict are `verify.cut_change_max_percent`.

## What to do

Make the two pictures either side of the cut continue, or drop `seamless = true` from the section so the cut dips through `[transition]`.

Troubleshooting says more under [A late landing](/help/troubleshooting#a-late-landing).
