The project declares a voice no turn of the script is read by.

This finding is a warning, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk check` reports it when it judges the project's files before a build.
Pass `--allow VOICE_UNUSED` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

## What to do

Hand a turn to the voice with a `[voice NAME]` line, or remove its `[voices.NAME]` table.

Troubleshooting says more under [A voice direction that names no voice](/help/troubleshooting#a-voice-direction-that-names-no-voice).
