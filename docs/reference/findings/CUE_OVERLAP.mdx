Two cues resolve close enough together that a viewer cannot tell them apart.

This finding is a warning, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk check` reports it when it judges the project's files before a build, and `decktalk cue` when it resolves each cue phrase against the takes.
Pass `--allow CUE_OVERLAP` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

## What to do

Move one of the two cues to a phrase further from the other, or shorten the first moment so it is over before the second begins.

Troubleshooting says more under [An unresolved cue](/help/troubleshooting#an-unresolved-cue).
