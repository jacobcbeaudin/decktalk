The change lands further from its word than verify.cue_offset_max_ms allows.

This finding is an error, and DeckTalk judges it itself. `decktalk verify` reports it when it measures the finished film and reads back each recording log.
Pass `--allow CUE_OFF` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

The settings that move this verdict are `verify.reference_lead_extra_ms`, `verify.onset_rise_points`, `verify.onset_diff_luma`, `verify.click_search_seconds`, `verify.click_floor_dbfs`, `verify.cue_offset_max_ms`, `verify.av_offset_max_ms`.

## What to do

Read how far off the message says the change landed. One late cue is usually a phrase on the wrong word, which `decktalk words` shows and `occurrence` picks, and a whole late section is usually motion on a clock of its own, which belongs in `DeckTalk.frame`.

Troubleshooting says more under [A late landing](/help/troubleshooting#a-late-landing).
