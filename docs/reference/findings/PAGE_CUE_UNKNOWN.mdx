A cue fired that no reveal, no handler and no slide in the deck declares, so nothing answered it.

This finding is an error, and the page reports it from the browser. `decktalk record` reports it when it records the pages, and `decktalk verify` when it measures the finished film and reads back each recording log.
Pass `--allow PAGE_CUE_UNKNOWN` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

## What to do

Name the cue in a moment attribute on the slide that plays it, or take its row out of `cues.json`.

Troubleshooting says more under [An unresolved cue](/help/troubleshooting#an-unresolved-cue).
