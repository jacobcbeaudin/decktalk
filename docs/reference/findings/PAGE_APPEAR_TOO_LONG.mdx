A line shown with data-words=appear holds more words than one cue can carry one at a time.

This finding is an error, and the page reports it from the browser. `decktalk record` reports it when it records the pages, and `decktalk verify` when it measures the finished film and reads back each recording log.
Pass `--allow PAGE_APPEAR_TOO_LONG` to carry on past it, and `--fail-on` to choose which severity fails a run.

## What to do

Shorten the line, or change its `data-words` from `appear` to `highlight`.

Troubleshooting says more under [A late landing](/help/troubleshooting#a-late-landing).
