## Landing error

`decktalk verify` decodes the finished film, finds the frame where each reveal starts to change, and
measures how far that frame sits from the word its cue names. A reveal passes when it lands within
`verify.cue_offset_max_ms` of its word plus half a frame, early or late, and the table grades every
reveal against `verify.cue_offset_max_ms` alone.

| Takes | Reveals | Median | Worst | Limit | Past the limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| placeholder | 36 | 43 ms | 59 ms | 80 ms | 0 |

A placeholder plays a click on every word of real word times, so its row measures the cue,
the recording and the film it is assembled into. It has no voice of its own to misplace a word, so a voiced take's timing
is not part of it.
