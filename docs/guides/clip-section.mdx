A clip section is a video file of your own that plays in the sequence with its own
audio. DeckTalk does not narrate it, and it scales and pads the picture to the frame
size. The scaffold ships with two. Section 4 is a clip between two page sections, and a
commented-out section 0 opens the video on camera before the first slide. The steps below
turn on section 0.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Uncomment section 0 in decktalk.toml">
    Remove the leading `# ` from the five lines of the clip section, and set the
    transition so that the cut out of the clip dips to black.

    ```toml decktalk.toml
    [[section]]
    number = 0
    title = "On camera"
    clip = "media/open.mp4"
    slate_seconds = 6       # a titled slate plays for this long when the file is missing

    [transition]
    dips = [[0, 1]]
    ```

    `dips` lists the boundaries that fade through black as `[from, to]` pairs of section
    numbers. The scaffold sets it to `[]`, which is straight cuts everywhere. Leave the
    key out to dip at every cut.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the heading to script.md">
    Every `[[section]]` needs a heading in the script, so add `## 0. On camera` above
    section 1. Nothing under it is spoken, because narration skips clip sections, so the
    body can be a stage direction that reminds you what plays there.

    ```md script.md
    ## 0. On camera

    [Your clip at media/open.mp4 plays here, with its own sound.]

    ## 1. Open
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drop the file in place, or build without it">
    Put the video at `media/open.mp4`. Git ignores `media/*.mp4` by default. A clip that
    is not 1920 by 1080 is scaled to fit and padded with black, and a clip with no audio
    track plays silent with a warning.

    The file does not have to exist when you build. A missing clip becomes a titled slate for
    `slate_seconds`, with the section title and the path where the file belongs, so the
    project builds before every asset does. The `--strict` flag on `build` or `assemble`
    turns a missing clip into an error instead.

    ```console
    decktalk build --silent
    ```

    ```text
    section 00: media/open.mp4 missing; slate for 6s (drop your clip at that path)
    [cut ] 00  slate -> 00-section.mp4  (6.000s)
    [cut ] 01  01-scene.webm (lead 1.4s trimmed) -> 01-section.mp4  (10.160s)
    ```

    The status report lists the clip with the others.

    ```text
      00  clip media/open.mp4                          cut
      01  deck/index.html?scene=1                  rec cut
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where a clip may sit

A clip may sit anywhere in the sequence. It can open the video before the first page
section, close it after the last one, or play between two page sections as B-roll. The
narration pauses for a clip between page sections and resumes on the first frame of the
page section after it, so every reveal still lands on its word.

The scaffold's section 4 is B-roll of this kind. Until a video exists at
`media/broll-chips.mp4`, a titled slate plays in its place for `slate_seconds`.

```toml decktalk.toml
[[section]]
number = 3
title = "How AI learns"
page = "deck/lesson.html"
scene = 3

[[section]]
number = 4
title = "Thousands of chips"
clip = "media/broll-chips.mp4"
slate_seconds = 4

[[section]]
number = 5
title = "The edit"
page = "deck/index.html"
scene = 4
```

The script has a `## 4. Thousands of chips` heading between sections 3 and 5, whose body
is a stage direction, and `cues.json` keys the cues of the page sections after the clip by
their section numbers, `"5"` and `"6"`. A section's `scene` names the scene in the page, so
section 5 still plays scene 4 and keeps the cue ids `4.1words` to `4.1moved`. Renumbering
the sections leaves the pages as they are. The scaffold sets no dip around the clip, so
both of its cuts are straight.

The narration track, `build/audio/narration.mp3`, still holds the spoken sections back to
back, and `timeline.json` gives their times in that track. The assembler splits the track
at each clip between page sections and starts each part where its first page section
starts in the video. The captions follow the same split, so no caption sits over the clip,
and the clip gets a chapter of its own. Replacing the clip file, or letting a slate stand
in for it, needs only `decktalk assemble`, because the narration never depends on the
clip's length.

`hold_seconds` is still allowed only on the last page section, and the load refuses it on
a page section before a clip.

## The clip's sound

The clip's own audio is mixed in at the section start, trimmed to its picture, and faded
over twenty milliseconds at both ends so that a cut never clicks. The narration is silent
while a clip plays, and the underscore ducks under a clip as it ducks under speech.
[Sound](/concepts/sound) describes the mix.
