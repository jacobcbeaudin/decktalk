InstrumentSans.woff2 is the variable font (weight axis 400-700) cut to the printable ASCII range plus a few punctuation marks with fontTools' subsetter; scripts/build_assets.py embeds it in the diagrams.

IBM Plex Mono, Copyright 2017 IBM Corp.
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
PlexMono.woff2 is the Medium (500) face cut the same way; scripts/build_assets.py embeds it in the diagrams.

Instrument Serif, Copyright 2022 The Instrument Serif Project Authors.
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
InstrumentSerif.woff2 is the regular face cut the same way; scripts/build_assets.py embeds it in the
two diagrams that carry a headline, and site/fonts/instrument-serif.woff2 is the same cut for the page.

Bricolage Grotesque, Copyright 2023 The Bricolage Grotesque Project Authors.
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
BricolageGrotesque.woff2 is a static instance (optical size 96, weight 500) cut the same way; scripts/build_assets.py traces the wordmark's letters from it, so it is never embedded as a face.

All four were fetched from Google Fonts as latin subsets and cut with fontTools' subsetter (pyftsubset).
