# __TITLE__: narration script

Each "## N." section is one cut of the video. Bracketed directions are not spoken: `[beat]` is a
short pause and anything else in brackets is a note to yourself.

---

## 1. A thing that travels

[Scene 1. A packet crosses the frame between two words, a ring turns, and a card springs in.]

This is a packet. [beat] It leaves when I say go, travels the whole width of the frame, and stops
dead on one word: now. [beat] A card follows it in on a spring, and comes to rest without a bounce.

## 2. A clock you can see

[Scene 2. A bar fills between two words while the voice keeps talking.]

Every frame is one second of the section clock. [beat] So a bar can fill from the first word all the
way to the last one, while the voice talks over it.

## 3. A picture from elsewhere

[Scene 3. A program draws the picture from the same word times, and the voice keeps going.]

This picture is drawn by another program, from the same word times. [beat] One bar for one, a
second for two, and a third for three, while the voice keeps going.
