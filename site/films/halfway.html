Each ## N. heading is one cut of the film. A [beat] is a short pause and is not spoken. The underlined words are cue phrases: a picture on the slide lands on each one.

The edit

Section 3 first said "eleven thousand". Changing it to "fourteen thousand" voiced and recorded that section again and kept the other three. The film before the edit is 0:39. The homepage plays the hero cut, taken before the edit, so it still says "eleven thousand", and it trims section 4 to its last two cues.

Halfway is not a product. It was written for this film, and its numbers are invented.
