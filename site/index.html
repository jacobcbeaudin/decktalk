How it works

One line, from the script to the film.

Twenty minutes for her. Twenty minutes for you.

1 of 5

You write the script.

2 of 5

Your voice reads it, with a time for every word.

3 of 5

A cue names the word each picture waits for.

4 of 5

The slide is plain HTML, and each picture carries its cue.

5 of 5

A browser films the slide, and every landing is measured.

Underlined phrases are cues. The small number is how many seconds into this section the picture appears. Click a word to move the stage there. Tab in, use the arrow keys, and press Enter to play from a word.

    Install
    
    
    Four commands to a first film.
    
    
    Needs Python 3.12 or later. Chromium and ffmpeg come with decktalk install, and the first build needs no key. Your own voice needs an ElevenLabs key and a voice id. Any ElevenLabs voice works, including a clone of your own.
    
    
      
      
    1. $ uv tool install decktalkInstalls the command.
      2. 
      
    2. $ decktalk installFetches Chromium and ffmpeg, once per machine. On Linux it asks for sudo once, for Chromium's system libraries.
      3. 
      
    3. $ decktalk init my-pitchWrites a starter script, cues, a slide page, decktalk.toml and .env.example.
      4. 
      
    4. $ cd my-pitch && decktalk build --no-voiceBuilds the film with placeholder narration and no key. Copy .env.example to .env, add your ElevenLabs key and voice id, and build again for your voice.
      5.
